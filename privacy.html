Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

Venture Atlas is made by Gabriel Viau. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Your trips stay yours

Trips, stops, notes, photos and settings are stored on your devices and in your own iCloud account (Apple’s private CloudKit database). The developer can’t see them. If you delete the app and your iCloud data, they’re gone.

Location, photos and camera

Location is used only when you ask for it (“Use my location”, “Add a stop here”) or when you turn on visit suggestions during a trip. Visits are kept on the device and never uploaded. Photo and camera access are used only to attach photos you choose to a stop, and to suggest stops from where your photos were taken. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Place search

When you search for a place while adding a stop, the text you type is sent to Apple Maps to find matching places. Towns and National Park Service sites built into the app are searched on the device.

Sharing trips

When you share a trip, Apple’s iCloud sharing gives the people you invite access to that trip. You can stop sharing at any time.

Friends (optional)

If you create a friends profile, your username, display name and friend code are stored in the app’s public iCloud database so other people can find you, along with the list of people you’ve added. Friends you both add see your trips read-only, through iCloud sharing; trips you hide from friends are never shared. Reports you send about another user are stored so the developer can review them.

You can delete your friends profile at any time from the Friends tab. That removes your username, friend code and friend list, and stops sharing your trips with friends.

No ads, no tracking

Venture Atlas has no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

Weather and maps

Weather forecasts come from Apple Weather (WeatherKit). Map data is built into the app from Natural Earth (public domain); on the Mac the map is drawn over Apple Maps.

Maps for places outside the US and Canada are optional downloads from this website (hosted by GitHub Pages), made when you ask for one. Like any website, GitHub sees the download request, including your IP address; nothing about you or your trips is sent. Downloaded maps stay on your device.
