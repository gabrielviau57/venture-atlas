Venture Plan

Plan the next road trip, together, on iPhone and Mac.

Lay out the route on the same map as Venture Atlas, then plan the details: days and drive times, where you’re staying (with campgrounds and prices from Recreation.gov), what to pack, what to book and when booking opens, the budget, and the weather, daylight and park alerts for each stop. Invite the people going so everyone can edit the plan. When you leave, send it to Venture Atlas; when you’re home, it goes into your atlas as a finished trip.

Your plans stay in your own iCloud, with no ads and no tracking.
