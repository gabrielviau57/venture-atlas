Common questions

My plans aren’t on my other device. Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud on, then open Settings in Venture Plan to see the iCloud status.

How do I plan with someone else? Open the plan, go to Plan together and choose Share plan. Everyone you invite can edit the plan, and each copy shows who edited it last. The owner can stop sharing at any time; anyone invited can leave.

How do I bring in a trip from Venture Atlas? Tap + and choose Import from Venture Atlas. Pick trips to copy as they are, or to start new plans like them. In Atlas, “Plan a trip like this” does the same for one trip.

What happens when the trip is over? By default the plan moves to Done the day after it ends and goes to Venture Atlas as a finished trip. You can change this in Settings, or for one plan under “When the trip is over”, and mark a plan finished yourself.

How do campgrounds get into the budget? On a stop, choose “Choose a campground nearby…” (or Stay here in Conditions). The campground and its nightly fee, when Recreation.gov lists one, fill in where you’re staying, and the budget counts it for each night. Fees are as published by Recreation.gov; check the campground’s page before you book.

Can I ask Siri? Yes: “What’s left to pack in Venture Plan”, “When’s my next trip in Venture Plan” and “What’s left to book in Venture Plan”. They’re also in the Shortcuts app.

Widgets and CarPlay. Add the Trip countdown or Packing and bookings widget from your Home Screen, Lock Screen or Mac desktop. In CarPlay, Venture Plan shows the plan under way (or the next one): the next stop, where you’re staying tonight and today’s stops, with directions in Apple Maps.

Does it work offline? The map, routes and planning tools work without a connection. Tap Update and save for offline in Conditions before you leave to keep weather, daylight and park information on the device.
