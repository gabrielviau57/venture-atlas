Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

Venture Plan is made by Gabriel Viau. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Your plans stay yours

Plans, stops, packing lists, bookings, budgets and settings are stored on your devices and in your own iCloud account (Apple’s private CloudKit database). The developer can’t see them. If you delete the app and your iCloud data, they’re gone.

Planning together

When you share a plan, Apple’s iCloud sharing gives the people you invite access to that plan, and they can edit it. Each copy shows who edited it last, using the name from that person’s Apple Account. You can stop sharing, or leave a plan someone shared with you, at any time.

Venture Atlas

If you also use Venture Atlas, the two apps work together through your own iCloud account and a folder the two apps share on your device. Sending a plan to Atlas copies it there; importing from Atlas reads your Atlas trips (read only); and places you’ve visited in Atlas are used to show “You’ve been here” on stops. Nothing goes through the developer.

Location

Location is used only when you ask for it (“Use my location” to add a stop). It isn’t tracked or kept apart from the stop you add.

Place search, weather and park information

When you search for a place, the text you type is sent to Apple Maps. Towns and National Park Service sites built into the app are searched on the device.

When you look at a plan’s conditions, the app sends each stop’s location (and date, for weather) to get information about it: weather from Apple Weather, park alerts, fees and hours from the National Park Service, campgrounds from Recreation.gov, and elevation from the US Geological Survey. Only the place and date are sent, never your name or your plans, and the results are saved on your device for offline use.

Widgets, Siri, Spotlight and CarPlay

Widgets, Siri answers (“What’s left to pack?”), Spotlight search and CarPlay read your plans on the device. Booking reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device.

No ads, no tracking

Venture Plan has no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking.
