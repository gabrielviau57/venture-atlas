Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email gabe.viau@gmail.com and include your device (iPhone, iPad, Mac) and what you were doing.

Common questions

My trips aren’t on my other device. Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive on, then open Settings in the app to see the sync status.

How do I share a trip? Open the trip and use the share button. The people you invite can view or edit it, depending on what you choose.

How do I delete my friends profile? Open the Friends tab and choose Delete Profile at the bottom. Your trips stay yours.

Can I back up my trips? Yes: Trips → … → Export saves a file you can keep or import later.
